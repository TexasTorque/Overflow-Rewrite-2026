# Overflow Rewrite 🔥

![Overflow](/overflow.jpeg)

## Subsystems
- Drivebase: Double Kraken X60 Swerve Drive (WCP Swerve X Flipped).
- Intake: Slap-down Intake with 1 NEO on the pivot and 2 NEOs on the roller.
- Hopper: 5 sequential polycarb tubes powered by a single Kraken X60, sloping down towards the feeder.
- Hopper walls: Passive extendable hopper walls with four bearings on two slides per side.
- Feeder: 2 polycarb rollers on the back and 3 on the front, bringing the balls up to the flywheel. Powered by two NEOs.
- Shooter: 9 Rev ION 4-inch Grip Wheels powered by 2 Kraken X60s. Servo-powered laser-only deployable hood.
- VISION: 4 Arducam OV9281s powered by a Jetson Orin Nano running a custom version of PhotonVision.
